One planning problem, three connected parts

Estimate travel time from past trips, assign orders to vehicles under constraints, then find a stop order that respects the order windows.

01 · ESTIMATE

Travel-time model

Uses trip history to estimate zone-to-zone travel duration for the selected hour.

02 · ASSIGN

CSP vehicle allocation

Chooses a van per order while respecting time conflicts and each van's capacity.

03 · ORDER

A* route search

Searches for the sequence of assigned stops with travel, waiting, and service time.

Presenter notes

Describe each order as a variable whose domain is the set of vans. CSP constraints remove assignments that violate capacity or a pairwise scheduling conflict. A backtrack means the solver undoes a choice and tries another.

Search

Route state tracks the current stop, completed orders, and time. The objective includes travel, waiting, and service duration.

Travel-time caveat

TLC taxi trip durations approximate travel between zones; they are not measured courier-van times.

Evaluation caveat

The model's MAE/RMSE evaluate held-out real taxi trips. They do not measure actual courier lateness or delivery performance.

Route arrivals are predictions based on the selected time model. The blue bars and scenario inputs are generated; they should not be presented as real delivery outcomes.
Presenter notes

Read the chart as a timeline: blue is the allowed window and the dot is the estimated arrival. A* searches feasible stop sequences using estimated travel times. Close by distinguishing model accuracy on taxi trips from delivery performance, which this dataset cannot establish.
